How it works

  1. Choose a paper and what to sit. The questions are picked at random from the revision pools, so every attempt is different.
  2. Turn off Wi-Fi and mobile data, then press Start. The questions only appear while you are offline, and the page goes full screen.
  3. Write against the timer. When time runs out, or you press Finish, your answers lock.
  4. Check every answer against the checklist written for that question. You must review each one before you can move on.
  5. Reconnect and download your work as a PDF, then hand it in or upload it to Google Classroom.
Before you start

Open this page while you are online so it loads fully. Do not refresh or close the page during the test. Your answers are saved on this device as you type, and leaving the page, leaving full screen or reconnecting are recorded on your PDF.

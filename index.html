The six concepts

Bias

How experience, preference or design distorts what is studied and how it is read.

Causality

Whether one thing really produces another, or they just happen together.

Change

How behaviour shifts, gradually or suddenly, by choice or by circumstance.

Measurement

How behaviour is turned into data, and what that data can and cannot capture.

Perspective

Why no single approach explains behaviour on its own.

Responsibility

The duties psychologists have to participants and to the public.

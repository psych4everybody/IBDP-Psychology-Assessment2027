What a psychology EE is

Your psychology EE is a review of published research. You analyse peer-reviewed studies on one focused question about behaviour or cognitive processes, build a clear argument, weigh the evidence that challenges it, and reach a reasoned judgement. You are not allowed to run your own experiments, interviews or surveys.

4,000words at most
About 40hours of your time
3reflection sessions with your supervisor
30marks across five criteria
A6Framework: question, research methods, structure
B6Knowledge and understanding
C6Analysis and line of argument
D8Discussion and evaluation
E4Reflection

From the IB extended essay guide and the psychology subject-specific guidance (first assessment 2027), paraphrased. Discussion and evaluation carries the most marks, so evaluation runs through the whole essay, not just the end.

Our example question
Followed through every stageTo what extent does taking notes by hand rather than on a laptop improve students' learning from lectures?

It is focused, about behaviour and cognition, and has a real debate behind it: a famous study, then replications that did not find the same result. Use it to practise, then apply each stage to your own question.

Model answers
Try first

Every exercise has a model answer underneath it. Have a real go at the exercise first, then compare: you learn far more from the gap between your answer and the model than from reading the model alone.

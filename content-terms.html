Animal researchBiological approachSo far only short examples, such as Harlow's monkeys in HD-05. No card yet on how animal research is used.

Studying non-human animals to understand behaviour, often because the procedure would be impossible or unethical with people. Findings have to be generalised to humans with care.

Research methodology

Not Section A content terms: Section A is never set on research methodology. You need these for Paper 2, the IA and, at HL, Paper 3.

ExperimentsResearch methodology

The researcher changes an independent variable and measures its effect on a dependent variable. In a true experiment participants are randomly allocated to conditions; in a quasi-experiment they are not.

Learn it in
CRM-03 What makes a study an experiment?CRM-04 Quasi and natural experiments
Also used in
CL-11 Class Practical: The Experiment
Practise
Paper 2: our experiment practical
ObservationsResearch methodology

Watching and recording behaviour as it happens, described by setting (naturalistic or controlled), awareness (overt or covert) and the observer's role (participant or non-participant).

Learn it in
CRM-05 What is an observation?CRM-05 Three dimensions of observation design
Also used in
HD-09 Class Practical: The Observation
Practise
Paper 2: our observation practical
Surveys and questionnairesResearch methodology

A fixed set of written questions answered by many people, using closed, rating-scale and open items.

Learn it in
CRM-06 Surveys and questionnaires
Practise

Interviews and focus groupsResearch methodology

Asking people questions in person: structured, semi-structured, or as a focus group where participants also respond to each other.

Practise

Correlational studiesResearch methodology

Measuring two or more co-variables as they already are, without changing either, to see whether and how strongly they are related.

Learn it in

Case studiesResearch methodology

An in-depth study of one person, group or event, usually combining several methods, often because the case is unusual.

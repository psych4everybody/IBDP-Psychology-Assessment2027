How to use it well

Answer before you look

Say it, or jot it in the box. Pulling an answer out of memory (retrieval practice) strengthens it far more than reading the answer again.

Rate yourself honestly

"Not yet" cards come back a few cards later and "Partly" cards come back at the end. Next time, Smart mix puts your weak cards first.

Little and often

One short session a day beats one long session the night before. The cards are mixed on purpose: switching topics is harder, and that is what makes it work.

How the worksheets work

1. Read the chapter here

Each chapter has a warm-up, the reading with two key studies, a self-check, a concept workout and exam practice.

2. Answer in your answer sheet

Every task has a number such as HD-05-C2. Write your answer under that number in the answer sheet your teacher shares with you.

3. Check against the marks

Use "What earns the marks" on each page, then the self-check box in your answer sheet.
